How to use this tool

This tool helps you estimate when to delegate a task to AI, collaborate with AI, or handle it manually. The decision depends on three things: how long the task takes you, how likely AI is to meet your standard, and how much time the full AI workflow will cost. In practice, better delegation and evaluation skills improve outcomes across all three variables.

Human baseline time

Human baseline time is the benchmark for your decision. It represents the full time required if you do the task yourself with no AI support. Longer tasks usually create more room for AI to add value, because even imperfect AI workflows can still save time overall. For short tasks, AI overhead (prompting, checking, retrying) can cancel out the benefit.

Probability of success

Probability of success reflects your confidence that AI will generate an acceptable result for this specific task. It varies by task type, context, and how clearly you define the goal. You can improve it by giving better instructions, setting clearer constraints, and using stronger evaluation and feedback loops. Domain expertise also increases this probability because you know what “good” looks like and where mistakes are likely to appear.

AI process time

AI process time includes the full cycle: writing the prompt, sending the request, waiting for the output, and checking whether the result is good enough. The model may respond quickly, but review and correction can still take significant time. This makes evaluation speed a key factor in whether delegation is worth it. Better prompts, clearer success criteria, and faster review methods can reduce AI process time substantially.

Result meanings

Autopilot

AI completes the task independently with minimal or no human intervention.

Collaboration

AI helps execute the task while you iterate multiple times to reach the desired output.

Manual

You complete the task yourself because AI cannot reliably do it, or the risk of incorrect output is too high.
